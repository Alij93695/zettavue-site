Zettavue

Zettavue is the private publishing tool of the ZettaVue Kids YouTube channel, a channel of original sing-along songs for young children.

What the app does

  • Uploads the channel owner's own song videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, and schedules when each one goes public.
  • Sets each video's title, description, thumbnail and "made for kids" setting.
  • Reads the channel's own video list and statistics, so it does not post a song twice and knows what to post next.

Who can use it

Zettavue is used only by the owner of ZettaVue Kids, on the owner's own computer, to manage that one channel. It is not offered to the public, and it never signs in to anyone else's Google account.
